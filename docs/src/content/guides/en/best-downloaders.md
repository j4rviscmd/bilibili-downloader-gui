## How to choose a downloader

Five checks before you commit:

1. **Safety**: ads and fake download buttons. Open-source apps can be audited by anyone
2. **Pricing**: truly free, subscription, or one-time purchase? "Free trials" often watermark or cap output
3. **Quality**: is it 480p-only, or does it handle 1080p/4K? (On Bilibili, quality also depends on login state)
4. **Subtitles**: can it embed or save subtitle files? Few downloaders can
5. **Batch downloads**: can it select and save bangumi episodes in bulk?

## Online tools (no installation)

### Tool D, Tool E, Tool F (browser-based)

Paste a URL, get a file. Maximum convenience, shared weaknesses:

- **Quality caps**: free tiers often stop at 480p–720p
- **Ads**: fake download buttons are common and can lead to dubious software
- **Privacy**: you send the URL of the video you watch to a third-party server
- **Missing features**: batch bangumi downloads and subtitle embedding are essentially unavailable

Fine for a one-off, low-quality save. For anything more, switch to a desktop app.

## Mobile apps

### Tool G and Tool H (mobile downloaders)

Tool G is free with ads; Tool H is paid. Mobile downloaders get pulled from app stores without warning when platform rules or Bilibili's site changes bite, so they rank last for stability. Subtitle embedding and bangumi batch downloads are generally unavailable.

Downloading on a PC and transferring to your phone is usually the calmer path.
