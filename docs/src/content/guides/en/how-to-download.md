## What is Bilibili?

Founded in 2009, Bilibili is a Chinese video sharing platform best known for its danmu (bullet comments) that scroll across the video, much like Nico Nico Douga. Its core content includes anime, bangumi (officially licensed dramas and anime series), gaming, music, and educational videos. For viewers outside China, Bilibili is often the only source for anime titles and VOCALOID music not distributed elsewhere.

Bilibili content falls into three broad types:

- **User-uploaded videos (UGC)**: videos posted by regular users
- **Bangumi**: officially licensed anime and dramas, organized into episodes
- **Courses**: paid and free lecture-style content split into multiple parts

A capable downloader handles all three. Note that some content is region-locked to mainland China and cannot be accessed from overseas at all.

## Before you download: copyright and terms of service

Downloading is only acceptable for **personal viewing**. Respect Bilibili's terms of service and copyright law, and avoid the following:

- Re-uploading or redistributing videos without the rights holder's permission
- Sharing saved paid content with others
- Any commercial use of saved videos

Everything in this guide assumes offline saving strictly for your own viewing.

## Method 3: Save with mobile apps

On iPhone/Android, the official Bilibili app offers downloads for some content, but availability is limited and playback stays inside the app. Third-party mobile downloaders exist but are frequently removed by app store review, so they are not a stable choice.

If you own a PC, saving on the desktop and transferring the file to your phone is the more reliable path.

## Troubleshooting download failures

Most failures come down to one of these:

1. **Region lock**: the content is mainland-China-only and cannot be fetched
2. **Wrong URL**: make sure you pasted the video page URL, not a playlist or homepage URL
3. **Expired login**: if high qualities are missing or bangumi fails, log in again
4. **Network errors**: the app retries automatically; if failures persist, wait and retry later

If nothing helps, report the issue on the GitHub repository's Issues page.
