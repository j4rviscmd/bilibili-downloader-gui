Bilibili is a video site, but much of its content is meant for listening: full-length music, radio shows, ASMR, and lectures. Saving audio only saves storage and makes commuting or studying easier. This guide shows how to save audio-only from Bilibili with the free [Bilibili Downloader GUI](/bilibili-downloader-gui/) app.

## When audio-only saving shines

- **Music**: VOCALOID tracks, C-pop, game theme songs in full length
- **Radio / stream archives**: talk-centric content where video adds nothing
- **ASMR / ambient**: pair with a sleep timer at night
- **Lectures / language learning**: audio is enough for review

There's no need to download the full video and convert it afterwards — the audio stream is saved directly.

## Steps to save MP3

1. Launch [Bilibili Downloader GUI](/bilibili-downloader-gui/) and paste the video URL
2. Choose "audio" as the save format
3. Start the download; the audio file lands in your output folder

Bangumi episodes and course parts can also be batch-saved as audio by selecting episodes first. The flow is the same as in the [main how-to guide](/bilibili-downloader-gui/guides/how-to-download/).

## About audio quality

- Quality follows the audio stream Bilibili serves for the chosen video quality (higher-quality videos usually carry higher-quality audio)
- Pick the highest available quality to get the best audio
- Reliable access to 1080p+ sources requires login (Firefox cookie / QR code)

## FAQ

### What does the saved file look like?

The audio stream is saved without merging video — playable directly in any player or music app.

### No subtitles or video?

Correct, audio-only contains neither. Use the video + subtitle mode if you need subtitles ([subtitles guide](/bilibili-downloader-gui/guides/download-with-subtitles/)).

### Audio won't save?

Check your login state. Without login, only lower-quality sources are available. Network errors retry automatically; if failures persist, try again later.
