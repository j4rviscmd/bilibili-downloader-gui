Bilibili is the center of anime streaming in the Chinese-speaking world, including titles never distributed elsewhere. Saving episodes one by one is a chore — this guide shows how to select episodes and batch download bangumi with the free [Bilibili Downloader GUI](/bilibili-downloader-gui/) app.

## What is bangumi?

Bangumi is Bilibili's umbrella for officially distributed anime, dramas, and movies. Each series is organized into episodes under a season page — similar to a series page on Western streaming sites.

- Each episode has its own URL, but the app can handle the whole series from the series URL
- Some content is paid (big-member exclusive); full access and high quality require login
- Course-style content also comes in multiple parts and works the same way

## Batch download steps (episode selection)

1. Open the bangumi series page (or any episode page) in your browser and copy the URL
2. Paste it into [Bilibili Downloader GUI](/bilibili-downloader-gui/) — the episode list for the series appears
3. Check the episodes you want (select-all works too)
4. Choose quality and, if needed, subtitles, then start the download
5. Episodes save one after another into your output folder

High quality (1080p and above) requires login via Firefox cookie or QR code. See the [main how-to guide](/bilibili-downloader-gui/guides/how-to-download/) for login steps.

## Combining with subtitles

Batch downloads support subtitle embedding at the same time:

- **Hard subtitles**: burned in, ready for phones and TVs
- **Soft subtitles**: saved as sidecar files for player-side switching

Subtitle details are in the [subtitles guide](/bilibili-downloader-gui/guides/download-with-subtitles/).

## Managing saved files

- The output folder is configurable in settings (Downloads by default)
- Long series produce many files; grouping one folder per series is recommended
- Network errors retry automatically, so long batches can run unattended

## Copyright notes

Bangumi includes official paid content. Keep saving strictly for **personal viewing**. Redistribution, sharing, or commercial use of saved files may constitute copyright infringement. Big-member episodes can only be saved within what your own account can watch.

## FAQ

### The episode list doesn't appear?

Check that the URL is from the bangumi series or an episode page — a plain user-uploaded video URL won't show a list. If it still fails, re-check your login.

### Why do some episodes fail?

Region-locked (mainland-China-only) or big-member episodes may not be fetchable depending on login and membership state.

### Can I save audio only?

Yes — batch audio-only saving works for bangumi too. See the [MP3 guide](/bilibili-downloader-gui/guides/download-mp3-audio/).
